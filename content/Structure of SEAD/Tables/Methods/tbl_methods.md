|171|Petrographic microscopy|A piece of the ceramic sherd is cut off, fastened to a microscope slide, ground down to 0,03 mm, and polished. The colour change during and after heating is noted and compared to the Munsell Colour Chart to determine the firing temperature and firing atmosphere of the archaeological sample. The samples are then once more heated above 1000 °C, in 50 °C intervals, until they reach their melting point. The assumption for the thermal analysis is that the colour change of the ceramic sample doesn't occur until after the original firing temperature has been reached.|Thermal|
