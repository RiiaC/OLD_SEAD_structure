>[!info] SEAD contains a variety of different data types, or domains. These include:  
>- Archaeobotany-Palaeobotany
>- Ceramic (Petrographic Microscopy)
>- Dendrochronology and wood analyses
>- Geoarchaeology (Soil analyses)
>- Palaeoentomology (Insect analysis)
>- Palynology (Pollen analysis)
>
>  *(The list of domains will, eventually, become links, as soon as we resolve some technical issues. In the meantime, please use the main web-page menu to navigate.)*
